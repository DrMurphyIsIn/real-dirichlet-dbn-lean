## 6. P6: the lead's source questions

**(a) 2610.27343's hypothesis and normalisation (READ).**
- H^L_t(z) = ∫_ℝ e^{tu²+izu}Φ_L(u)du, with H^L_0(z) = ξ_L((1+iz)/2). That is the same s = ½ + iz/2 map as Polymath's ζ convention.
- With z = x + iy, Re s = (1 − y)/2.
- The initial-strip hypothesis (3.3) is H^L_0(x+iy) ≠ 0 for |y| > D.
- **Remark 3.1 says it outright:** "the zero-free half-plane Re s > 7/8 asserted in [24, Theorem 1.1], together with the
  functional equation, would imply the initial strip with D = 3/4. The conclusions reported for this value of D are conditional
  on that global strip hypothesis."
- **Remark 3.4:** "The initial strip hypothesis alone gives Λ_L ≤ D²/2 by Theorem 2.6". That is 9/32 at D = 3/4.
- [24] is OpenAI's 7/8 preprint (30 Sep), and [23] is OpenAI's 11/12 preprint. The paper also lists 11/12 ⇒ D = 5/6.
- **So 7/8 ⇒ |Im z| ≤ 3/4 in their variable ⇒ Λ_L ≤ 9/32: CONFIRMED.**

**(b) The scope of OAI's Dirichlet 7/8 (READ; 4870cf09).**
- `∀ {q} [NeZero q] (χ : DirichletCharacter ℂ q) {s}, (7/8 : ℝ) < s.re → ¬(χ = 1 ∧ s = 1) → DirichletCharacter.LFunction χ s ≠ 0`,
  on Mathlib's LFunction.
- It covers every modulus and every character, primitive or not, of any order. It is a fixed half-plane with no constants,
  uniform in the conductor.
- It was kernel-replayed in the programme on 10-07 (family-003 challenges, Comparator with nanoda, both kernels). The manuscript
  is not refereed.

**(c) de Bruijn for Φ_L.**
- The paper's Theorem 2.6 ("essentially contained in Theorem 13 of [9]", de Bruijn, Duke 1950; "see also [11]", Dobner) is
  stated for H^L_t in its setting: primitive real χ, m ≥ 3. The m ≤ 400 restriction applies only to Theorem 1.1's numerical Λ*
  part.
- The inputs, proved in the paper:
  - Φ_L is real and even ((2.10) and Lemma 2.5), via the root number W(χ) = +1 for real primitive χ (Lemma 2.9);
  - Φ_L decays superexponentially, |Φ_L(u)| ≤ C_m exp((1+2a)|u| − (π/m)e^{4|u|}) (2.11).
- **Positivity of Φ_L is not needed** for the strip contraction, and the paper does not claim it. It notes that H^L_0 need not
  be positive on iℝ, and uses Platt only for the Λ* improvements.
- bb did not re-read de Bruijn 1950. Its hypotheses are CITED-SECOND, via 2610.27343.

**Consequence: the label needs correcting.**
- "OAI 7/8 + de Bruijn ⇒ Λ_L ≤ 9/32 for every primitive real χ" is valid. But it is **2610.27343's own conditional statement**

- What is ours: the hypothesis is discharged by a kernel-replayed Lean theorem, stated on Mathlib's LFunction, with the

  - e.g. m = 397: the unconditional pair gives 0.29782, against 9/32 at D = 3/4 (Table 10).
